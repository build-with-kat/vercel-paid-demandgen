02 — Idea two

ABM: your list and content, paid's reach

Demand gen and sales own the target accounts and the material. Paid gets it in front of the decision makers and champions at those companies, and scores each account so budget goes where it can actually close.

Input

Target account list

From demand gen and sales, aligned to the current play

→
Enrich and score

Fit
Size, industry, tech stack, and whether teams there already build on Vercel

Signal
Anyone from the company engaging: site visits, ad engagement, event registrations, product usage

→
Output

Assign a tier

A = fit and signal
B = fit, no signal yet
C = weak fit

↓
TIER A · FIT AND SIGNAL
Work the buying group

LinkedIn by title, exec and technical split. Retargeting. Higher Google bids.

CTA: your guide, webinar, or workshop. Sales alerted.

TIER B · FIT, NO SIGNAL
Build coverage

Same targeting, lower frequency. Awareness and event registration.

CTA: register, read, watch. First engagement moves them to A.

TIER C · WEAK FIT
Stop spending

Nothing runs. Added to exclusion lists on LinkedIn and Google.

Revisited quarterly.

Ship SF is where I'd run it first

October 15. Tier A accounts get invited to the executive sessions, Tier B gets registration campaigns by role. Afterwards, attendees, no-shows, and recap viewers get different follow-up, and accounts where several people engaged move up a tier and go to sales. Same structure then runs against every webinar and event after it.

How I'd measure it

  • Coverage. Share of Tier A and B accounts reached, and roles per account.
  • Movement. Accounts moving from B to A. The earliest read that the message works, months before pipeline lands.
  • Conversion. Meetings, opportunities, and pipeline from Tier A, paid-sourced and paid-influenced reported separately.
North star

Pipeline created or influenced from Tier A accounts.
